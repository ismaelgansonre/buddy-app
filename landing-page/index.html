Meet Buddy

Not an app. A friend.

🏠

Lives on your dock

A tiny pixel character that walks along your dock, jumps between apps, and hangs out while you work. He's just... there. Like a good friend.

💬

Talk to him

Click the little Spider-Man and start talking. Ask him anything. Vent about your code. Get help with a problem. He actually listens and says something useful back.

👀

Sees your screen

With permission, Buddy peeks at what you're doing and drops casual comments. "oh that's a clean design" or "you've been on this file for a while."

What he can do

Small character.
Surprisingly useful.

🎙

Voice Mode

Hold to talk. He listens. He responds with his own voice. Like talking to a friend sitting next to you. 100% private, everything stays on your Mac.

🎯

Focus Guardian

Set a focus session. Buddy guards it. Opens Twitter? He'll gently remind you. Finish without distractions? He celebrates.

💧

Health Reminders

Water. Posture. Breaks. Eye rest. He cares about you more than you do. Five different reminder types with cute animations.

🔍

Stuck Detector

Been staring at the same file for 20 minutes? Switching apps frantically? Buddy notices and offers to help.

😄

20 Emotions

Tap him. He reacts. Tap more. He escalates. Happy, sad, angry, love, sleepy, celebrating. He's got range.

🧠

Memory

He remembers your conversations, learns your work patterns, and gets more helpful over time. Your context stays local.
